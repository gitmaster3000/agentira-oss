# Navigating Agentira

A short tour of the two things that are always on screen: the **top bar** and
the **sidebar**.

## The top bar

Running along the top, left to right:

- **The logo** ("Acme Inc / Agentira") — click it any time to get back Home.
- **The project switcher** — the pill next to `PROJECT` shows which project
  you're in. Its colour chip matches the project, and a blue pulsing dot with a
  number appears when that project has agents running right now. Click the pill
  to open **Switch project**, pick another project, or create a new one from the
  bottom of the list.
- **The breadcrumb** — where you are, e.g. *Atlas › Board*.
- **Search** — click it (or press `⌘K`) to jump to anything.
- **The running pill** — *"4 running"* means four agent runs are in flight.
  Click it to slide out **Pulse**, the live panel of what your agents are doing.
  When nothing is running it reads *"Live status"*.
- **New** — create a task, an epic, or a project.
- **The bell** — notifications. A red dot means something is unread.

## The sidebar

The sidebar is grouped into three parts:

- **Top** — Home, Inbox, My Work, Chat. These follow you everywhere.
- **BUILD** — Agents, Runs, Conductor, Agent Runtimes, MCP Servers. Your agent
  infrastructure. The Runs row shows a live count when runs are in flight.
- **PROJECT** — Overview, Board, Backlog, Roadmap, Workflow, Deploy, Settings.
  These always apply to the project currently selected in the top bar; switch
  project up there and this whole group follows.

At the bottom is your user row — click it for Settings, Profile, and Sign out.

## Collapsing the sidebar

The button at the **top of the sidebar** collapses it down to a narrow strip of
icons, giving the page more room. Click it again to bring the labels back.

While collapsed:

- Hover any icon and its name appears as a tooltip, so you can still navigate
  by feel.
- The BUILD and PROJECT headings turn into thin divider lines with a small
  icon, so you can still see where one group ends and the next begins.
- Your user menu opens out to the side rather than above.

On a phone or a narrow window the sidebar tucks away entirely. Tap the ☰ button
in the top bar to slide it in, and tap outside it to dismiss. It always shows
full labels there, however you left it on desktop.
